Information We Collect

We collect limited personal information when you create an account on the stock trading simulator. This may include a username, email address, and login-related information. We also collect simulated trading data such as transaction history, portfolio holdings, and cash balances.

How We Use Your Information

We use this information only to operate the trading simulator, authenticate users, display portfolios and trade activity, and allow administrators to manage stocks, market hours, and simulated market behavior.

TraderSim is a simulation platform only. All trades, prices, balances, and market activity are fictional and provided for educational or demonstration purposes.
